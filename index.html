    About AgriScan AI

    AgriScan AI is an intelligent web-based platform that revolutionizes plant disease diagnosis through advanced machine learning algorithms. Simply upload or capture an image of a plant leaf, and receive instant, accurate identification of diseases along with comprehensive prevention measures and treatment recommendations.

    How It Works

    1

    Upload Image

    Select a clear, well-lit image of the affected leaf from your device or capture one directly using your camera for immediate analysis.

    2

    AI Analysis

    Our advanced neural network processes the image in seconds, analyzing thousands of visual patterns to detect disease signatures with high accuracy.

    3

    Get Results

    Receive instant diagnosis with disease identification, severity assessment, and actionable prevention and treatment recommendations.
